Math Box · Why paste matters
ΔT=P×Rth
temperature rise = heat (W) × thermal resistance (°C per W)
With paste: 142 × 0.30 = 42.6 → 25 + 42.6 ≈ 68 °C
No paste: 142 × 0.60 = 85.2 → 25 + 85.2 ≈ 110 °C
Past the ~95 °C limit, so the CPU throttles.
