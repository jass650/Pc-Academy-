Math Box · Amps per pin, I = P ÷ V
8-pin PCIe: 150 W ÷ 12 V = 12.5 A ÷ 3 pins = 4.2 A per pin
8-pin
12V-2x6: 600 W ÷ 12 V = 50 A ÷ 6 pins = 8.3 A per pin
12V-2x6
Twice the current per pin. That's why a half-seated plug can overheat.
Math Box · Fan headers
One header: 1 A × 12 V = 12 W
Five fans: 5 × 3 W = 15 W ÷ 12 V = 1.25 A
green = what one header can supply (1 A)
1.25 A > 1 A, so split fans across headers or use a hub
