Math Box · Why two sticks?

DDR5-6000 bandwidth

Each channel moves 64 bits = 8 bytes per transfer
6000 million/s × 8 bytes = 48 GB/s per channel
48 GB/s × 2 channels = 96 GB/s
One stick
48 GB/s
Two sticks
96 GB/s

Math Box · Why the top slot?

PCIe 4.0 × 4 lanes

16 GT/s × 128/130 = 15.75 Gb/s
× 4 lanes = 63 Gb/s
÷ 8 bits = ~7.9 GB/s
A PCIe 3.0 slot halves it to ~3.9 GB/s.
The top slot connects straight to the CPU.
